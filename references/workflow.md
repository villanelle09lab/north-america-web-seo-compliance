# Workflow, inputs, and stage gates

Use this reference when planning or running the end-to-end workflow.

## Stage 0 intake

Ask only for fields not already verified in the current project. Group missing inputs into one compact request.

### Identity and market

- Product/site name and canonical Web domain.
- Legal entity, registration details, mailing address, and public support/privacy/copyright/safety contacts.
- Target countries, US states, Canadian provinces, languages, and excluded locations.
- Merchant of Record and contracting entity when payments exist.

### Product and content

- Core product: content, AI character, creator platform, video/live, generation tool, or combination.
- Content rating and restrictions.
- Content source: internal production, licensed, contracted creators, open UGC, or generated by users.
- Real-person involvement: photo, face, voice, motion, name, likeness, celebrity, or reference input.
- Public UGC, comments, publishing, messaging, sharing, creator monetization, and moderation model.
- Age/access restriction and regional behavior, if any.

### Data, vendors, and monetization

- Anonymous viewing, accounts, OAuth, email/phone verification, chat logs, prompts, media, history, favorites, profiles, IP/device data, and sensitive data.
- Analytics, ads, CMP, CDN/video, authentication, email/SMS, payments, access restriction, customer support, and safety vendors.
- Retention periods, storage region, cross-border transfers, sale/share/targeted-advertising position, and GPC handling.
- Free, subscription, auto-renewal, one-time purchase, virtual currency, tips, creator split, refunds, trials, affiliate, sponsorship, advertising, and marketing email/SMS.
- Web, PWA, iOS, Android, and wallet-connected surfaces.

### Intake states

Use exactly these states:

- `Confirmed`: backed by the user or project evidence.
- `Working assumption`: useful for drafting but not publishable as fact.
- `Unconfirmed`: required input is missing.
- `Not applicable`: reviewed and inapplicable, with reason.
- `Launch blocker`: missing or unresolved information prevents safe release.

Do not advance if product identity, jurisdiction, content/UGC model, real-person involvement, or monetization is materially ambiguous.

## Stage 1 sample selection

Score candidates from 1 to 5, then apply these default weights:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| North American or English-market relevance | 25% |
| Verifiable Web/search visibility | 20% |
| Similarity to the target business model | 20% |
| SEO/content infrastructure maturity | 20% |
| Compliance and governance coverage | 15% |

The score orders research; it does not prove compliance or market rank. Separate each site's comparison role:

- `Direct product reference`
- `Scale/SEO reference`
- `Safety/governance reference`
- `Regulatory counterexample`
- `Reserve candidate`

When reliable traffic tooling is unavailable, say so and use bounded indicators such as official product scale claims, indexed template breadth, app-market evidence, or reputable market reports. Never fabricate visits, rankings, or keyword volume.

## Stage 2 minimum evidence set

For each site, attempt to capture:

1. Homepage, footer, About, and Contact.
2. `robots.txt`, sitemap index, canonical, hreflang, status behavior, and representative schema.
3. Category/list, detail/content, author/editorial, blog/help, signup, and pricing templates.
4. Terms, Privacy, Cookie, subscription/cancellation/refund, acceptable use/community rules, and accessibility statement if present.
5. DMCA/copyright, content removal, impersonation/synthetic-media, child safety, law-enforcement, complaints, and appeals.
6. Age/access restriction, structured metadata, and regional blocks where relevant.
7. Cookie choices, Do Not Sell or Share, privacy request form, GPC behavior where observable, marketing opt-in, and consent withdrawal.
8. Checkout disclosure, trial, renewal, cancellation, refund, dispute, and virtual-currency behavior when relevant.

Evidence status must be one of:

- `Verified`
- `Not found in reviewed evidence`
- `Blocked/inaccessible`
- `Authentication required`
- `Not applicable`
- `Needs human reproduction`

## Stage 3 official-source baseline

Use current primary sources. Depending on scope, check:

- US federal statutes and regulator guidance, including the FTC and Copyright Office.
- Applicable US state privacy, consumer-protection, subscription, age-restriction, and content-safety authorities.
- Canadian federal sources and applicable provincial privacy/consumer authorities.
- Google Search Central and any relevant product/platform policies.
- W3C/WCAG and applicable accessibility authority or counsel analysis.
- Payment processor, app-store, CMP, email/SMS, or vendor rules when those systems are in scope.

Create a jurisdiction matrix instead of writing a universal `North America requires X` statement. For unsettled or disputed laws, record effective date, litigation/enforcement status if verified, implementation assumption, and a counsel question.

Handle these distinctions carefully:

- CCPA/CPRA terminology is not a synonym for every US state privacy law.
- PIPEDA does not erase provincial private-sector privacy rules.
- A click-through age confirmation is not automatically adequate age assurance.
- WCAG conformance and ADA legal exposure are related but not identical claims.
- E-E-A-T is an evaluation concept, not a standalone statutory duty or simple schema checklist.

## Human review gates

At each gate, give the reviewer a short checklist:

### After Stage 0

- Are business facts correct?
- Are real-person inputs and UGC boundaries accurate?
- Are jurisdictions and monetization complete?
- Which placeholders are launch blockers?

### After Stage 1

- Are official domains verified?
- Are sites correctly classified?
- Is each site useful as direct, SEO, governance, or risk evidence?
- Is any category padded with weak or misclassified candidates?

### After Stages 2–3

- Are core pages or flows missing?
- Did any blocked page get described as absent?
- Was competitor practice mistaken for law?
- Are laws and guidance current and jurisdiction-specific?

### After Stage 4

- Does every row apply to the actual product?
- Are facts and placeholders honest?
- Is mandatory copy original and operationally true?
- Is a counsel question created for every unresolved legal judgment?
- Does every P0 item have an owner and test?

## Change triggers

Reopen Stage 0 and re-run affected stages when any of these change:

- New country, state, province, or language.
- Paid subscription, auto-renewal, virtual currency, ads, affiliate, or sponsorship.
- UGC, public comments, messaging, creator tools, or uploads.
- Real-person faces, voices, motion, names, likenesses, or reference media.
- New vendor, new sensitive data, new retention period, or new cross-border transfer.
- New AI generation mode or material content-policy change.
- New or materially changed law, regulator guidance, search policy, or payment-platform rule.
